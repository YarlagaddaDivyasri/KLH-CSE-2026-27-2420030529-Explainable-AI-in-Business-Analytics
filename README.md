## Abstract

This project focuses on applying Explainable AI (XAI) to improve transparency and trust in business analytics. Three benchmark datasets—Bank Marketing, Telco Customer Churn, and Online Retail—are used to evaluate different real-world business scenarios. Multiple machine learning models, including Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost, and CatBoost, are trained and compared based on their predictive performance. SHAP, LIME, PDP, ICE, Permutation Feature importance are used to explain model predictions and identify important features, considering factors such as fidelity and sparsity. Finally, a unified comparative framework evaluates accuracy, explainability, and computational effectiveness to recommend the most suitable ML–XAI combination for business decision-making.

## Objectives

- To develop machine learning models for analyzing and predicting outcomes from benchmark business datasets.
- To integrate SHAP and LIME techniques to provide clear and meaningful explanations of model predictions. 
- To enhance model transparency and interpretability for better understanding of business-related predictions. 
- To evaluate and compare model performance and explainability to identify the most effective approach.
